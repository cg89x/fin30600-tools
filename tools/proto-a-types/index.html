How to use: pick a resting order type, set its limit or trigger price, and read the price path left to right: the red dashed line is your order, the gold dot the first bar it executes — if it ever does. “New path” redraws the market. Drag the price toward or away from the path and watch the trade-off: a market order executes for sure at an uncertain price; a limit or stop order names its price but may never execute — and a stop can gap.
