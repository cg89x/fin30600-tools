The limit order book

Gold bars are resting sell orders (asks), blue bars resting buy orders (bids); bar length is the number of shares available at that price — the “market depth.” A market order consumes the book from the best price outward.

Order types — limit & stop orders

The definitions

Market order:
buy or sell at the best available price — immediate execution, but price risk.
Limit buy:
buy at or below a specified price (the limit price).
Limit sell:
sell at or above the limit price.
Stop-loss order:
sell if the price falls below a specified level.
Stop-buy order:
buy if the price rises above a specified level.

Limit orders “limit” price risk and stop orders “stop” losses: both remove price uncertainty, but execution is not guaranteed.

B · A resting order against a moving price

Price path Your limit / trigger price First bar the order executes

Limit orders fill at the limit price or better. Stop orders only trigger a market order at the crossing price — on a gap (a ±$1 jump in this path), execution can be materially worse than the trigger.
