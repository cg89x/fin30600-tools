The limit order book is other people’s resting limit orders. Rest one of your own and it joins the queue; send a market order and it eats the book; arm a stop and it fires a market order into whatever depth is left.

The limit order book — everyone’s resting orders, including yours

Gold bars are resting sell orders (asks), blue bars resting buy orders (bids). Your limit order is the hatched tail of its level: everything ahead of it must trade first (price–time priority). The five levels nearest the spread are shown per side.

Prices only move because orders trade against the book. A “gap” is one market order eating several price levels in a single tick — exactly what a stop order fires into.
